Cookie policy

What this website stores on your device, what third parties may store, and how to change your choice at any time.

Last updated 16 November 2026

Our approach

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts and images are served from our own site. We ask before loading any content from other companies.

What we store

When you make a choice in the cookie banner, we save it in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask again on every page.

NamePurposeTypeDuration
ncs-abuja-consentRemembers whether you chose “Allow all” or “Essential only”.Essential, local storageUntil you clear your browser data

Third-party content

Some pages can show content from other services. These services may set their own cookies and collect data under their own policies. They only load if you choose “Allow all”, or when you press play or “Show map” on a specific item.

ServiceWhereWhy
YouTube (Google), privacy-enhanced modeHome and Events pagesPlays the Techies’ Hangout video. Google privacy policy
Google MapsContact pageShows the location of the Abuja office. Google privacy policy

Links to other websites, such as ncs.org.ng or Google Drive documents in our library, are governed by those websites’ own policies.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page, or right here.

You can also clear stored data and cookies in your browser settings. If you do, we will ask for your choice again.
