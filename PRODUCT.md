# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no build step (existing codebase). Shared header/footer rendered by `site-shell.js`.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):
- IT professionals in the FCT considering NCS membership or attending chapter programmes.
- Government institutions, partners and sponsors evaluating the chapter as a credible professional body to engage.

## Product Purpose

Public website for the Nigeria Computer Society (NCS) Abuja Chapter. It presents the chapter's mission, leadership, programmes, initiatives, advocacy record and documents, and routes visitors to membership (via the national NCS platform) or to the chapter by email. Success: a visitor understands who the chapter is within one screen and takes one action (join, contact, partner).

## Positioning

The capital-city chapter of Nigeria's umbrella IT professional body (founded 1978 as COAN, NCS since 2002), physically close to the federal institutions it engages (NDPC, NIMC, PTDF, NITDA, Nigeria Police Force ICT).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend: membership registration happens on ncs.org.ng; all chapter enquiries go to email.
- Embeds: YouTube (privacy-enhanced), Google Maps, Google Fonts. A cookie policy and consent banner is required (confirmed request).
- No event feed exists; upcoming dates are requested by email.

## Brand Commitments

- Official NCS Abuja logo (`assets/cropped-ncs-abuja-logo-sml.png`) and NCS green identity are kept.
- User-supplied references (binding): `sample image.webp` for layout feel (white header, oversized display word layered behind the hero subject, floating stat card, warm-neutral band, black section with white rounded strip); colour references `best color grade.JPG`, `best color grade 2.JPG`, `green color sample background 1.JPG` (vivid green, deep green-black, halftone dots, thin gold lines).
- No em dashes in site copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real chapter photography in `assets/` (advocacy visits 2022 to 2026, RISE 2026, NITDA forum, Techies' Hangout), executive portraits in `assets/team/` (480px only).
- Real facts: 1978 founding, 2002 rename, 9 membership grades, 4 monthly programmes, 5 initiatives, 12 executives, 4 July 2026 news posts.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: member counts, ratings, testimonials, quotes, partner logos (only names), upcoming event dates.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over hype: every claim is a documented fact.
2. Show the people and the rooms: real photography carries the story.
3. One clear next step per section: join, contact or partner.
4. Respect visitors' privacy: third-party embeds load only with consent.
